What is a calorimeter ?
▪Calorimeters  are used  to measure  the energy  
of particles  : 
▪1- Neutral  particles  
▪2- High  energy  charged  particles  
▪Primary  tool for jet energy  measurement
▪ Used to measure  the energy  of particle,  
crucial  for reconstructing  the total  jet energy  
(EJET=EECAL+EHCAL).
▪“Typical”  jet: 
~62% charged  particles  (mainly  hadrons)  
~27% photons  
~10% neutral  hadrons
~1% neutrinos     (Source)
2Why do we need  high granularity calorimeter ?
▪FCC  will study  physics  at energy  scale  of “tens of TeV” .
▪Jets will be highly  boosted  at such  energy  scale . 
▪High  jet energy  resolution  is essential, also for Higgs  factories  >90% of events  
have  >2 jets. So having  excellent  JER will help  study  particles  and their  
interactions  
▪High  granularity  improves  pile-up robustness
▪Role  evolved  after  following  developments :
▪Particle  Flow  Algorithm : Reconstructs  individual  particle  using  the most  precise  
subdetector .
▪Boosted  Jet Tagging : It requires  fine substructure  resolution  in jets.

6High Level Features
▪Two kinds of features were developed:
▪Global Features : Describe overall shower characteristics independent of calorimeter  
granularity .
▪Example: Energy deposited in full calorimeter.
▪Local Features: Capture shower properties using low-level information from individual cells .
▪Example: Radius of hadronic shower.
